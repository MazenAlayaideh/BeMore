The BeMore Manifesto

01

Attention is the raw material.

Lectures demand hours; attention arrives in minutes. BeMore compresses the gap — indexing every claim, diagram, and pause so focus is spent on understanding, never on scrubbing a timeline.

02

Context beats memory.

Re-watching is a tax on the curious. Semantic search and timestamped notes let you return to the exact second a concept clicked — so recall becomes retrieval, and retrieval becomes instant.

03

Learning is a conversation.

Flashcards quiz you back. The AI tutor answers in context. Analytics tell instructors where minds stall. BeMore turns one-way broadcasts into a dialogue that never sleeps.
